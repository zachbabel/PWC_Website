2027 PWC Winter 3rd-6th Folkstyle Session

PWC offers a Winter session for wrestlers of all skill levels in grades K-6th. This session is for athletes enrolled in grades 3rd through 6th. There will be multiple opportunities for competition experience throughout the session and while strongly encouraged, it is not required.

Eligibility: Be enrolled in grades 3-6 during the 2026/2027 School Year

Coaches: Brad Bryon, Jonathan Lucha, Jack Martin

Session Dates: January 11 - February 24

Practice Days: Mondays and Wednesdays

Practice Time:

Mondays & Wednesdays: 6:45PM-8:00PM

Practice Location:

Kenilworth Junior High 800 Riesling Rd, Petaluma, CA 94954

Tournaments: PWC plans to attend and have coaching available for our athletes at several Sonoma County tournaments as well as the State Championships in Fresno

Cost: $175

Registration: via WrestlingIQ

 

*a USA Wrestling Athlete Membership purchased separately through USA Wrestling is also required to participate which costs $70. It can be obtained at this link.

For scholarship inquiries, please email petalumawc@gmail.com. Petaluma Wrestling Club believes that all individuals should have access to youth wrestling.

 

PWC Cancellation/Refund Policy:

  • Cancellation before session starts: 100% refund
  • Cancellation up to two weeks after the start of the session: 50% refund
  • Cancellation after 2 weeks (unless compelling circumstances): No refund
