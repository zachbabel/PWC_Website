2026 PWC Spring K-3rd Freestyle Session

PWC offers a Winter session for wrestlers of all skill levels in grades K-3rd.  This session is for athletes enrolled in grades Kindergarten through 3rd grade.  There will be multiple opportunities for competition experience throughout the session and while strongly encouraged, it is not required.

Eligibility: Be enrolled in grades K-3 during the 2025/2026 School Year

Coaches: Danny Dardon, David Arvizo, Jack Martin

Session Dates:  March 24 - May 28

Practice Days: Tuesdays and Thursdays

Practice Times: 5:00PM-6:00PM

Practice Location: Petaluma Fairgrounds Expo Hall - 175 Fairgrounds Dr, Petaluma, CA 94952

Tournaments: PWC plans to attend and have coaching available for our athletes at several tournaments

Cost: $175

Registration: via WrestlingIQ

 

*a USA Wrestling Athlete Membership purchased separately through USA Wrestling is also required to participate which costs $70. It can be obtained at this link.

For scholarship inquiries, please email petalumawc@gmail.com. Petaluma Wrestling Club believes that all individuals should have access to youth wrestling. 

 

PWC Cancellation/Refund Policy:

  • Cancellation before session starts: 100% refund
  • Cancellation up to two weeks after the start of the session: 50% refund
  • Cancellation after 2 weeks (unless compelling circumstances): No refund
